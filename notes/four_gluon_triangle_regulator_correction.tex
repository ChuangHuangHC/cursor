\section*{Organisational principle}

The note builds every $\partial_t$ equation in two layers:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Topology + external-leg distribution.}
        All one-loop graphs that contribute to a given $n$-point function are
        classified by vertex content; within each class every inequivalent
        assignment of the external legs to the loop is written once as a
        $\Lambda$ diagram (no regulator).
  \item \textbf{Regulator insertion.}
        The flow $\partial\Lambda$ is obtained by putting $\otimes$ on one
        internal line, always routed so that this line carries the loop
        momentum $q$.  Insertions that differ only by a shift of $q$ are
        \emph{not} written out; they are absorbed into a multiplicity in
        $\partial_t\lambda$.  Insertions that change which vertices bound the
        regulated line are inequivalent and must appear as separate diagrams
        (cf.\ $\partial\Lambda^{\bar{c}cA}_{a_1,a_2,a_3}$ and
        $\partial\Lambda^{\bar{c}cA}_{b_1,b_2,b_3}$).
\end{enumerate}

For the gluon triangle (one $A_4$ and two $A_3$) the three internal lines fall
into two inequivalent classes:

\begin{itemize}
  \item two $A_4$--$A_3$ lines (related by $q\to -P+q$, where $P$ is the
        total momentum through the $A_4$), multiplicity $2$;
  \item one $A_3$--$A_3$ line, multiplicity $1$.
\end{itemize}

These two classes cannot be represented by a single $\partial\Lambda$ times $3$.
The total triangle weight remains $2\times 6 + 1\times 6 = 18$, so the
one-loop counting $24+3+18+12+24$ is unchanged; only the bookkeeping of the
flow is corrected.

for every class all inequivalent distributions of the external legs over the

$\partial\Lambda^{4A}_{g_1,\ldots,l_1}$ with multiplicity $2$, the latter as
$\partial\Lambda^{4A}_{g_2,\ldots,l_2}$ with multiplicity $1$.
\end{quote}

\section{Replacement text (start of the flow)}

Replace the sentence that introduces~\eqref{eq:replace-rule} / original~(112)
by:

\begin{quote}
\small
now turn to the flow equation. the regulator insertion $\otimes$ is put on
the internal line carrying the loop momentum $q$, i.e.\
$(\partial\Lambda^{4A}_{x})$ is obtained from $(\Lambda^{4A}_{x})$ by the
replacement of the propagator of that line by its $\tilde{\partial}_t$
derivative,
\begin{equation}
  G_c(q)\to\mathrm{d}G_c(q),\qquad
  \Pi^\perp(q)\,G_A(q)\to\Pi^\perp(q)\,\mathrm{d}G_A(q),\qquad
  G_q(q)\to\mathrm{d}G_q(q),
  \label{eq:replace-rule}
\end{equation}
all other propagators being unchanged. for the gluon triangles the two
inequivalent choices of that line are written separately below
($x=g_1,\ldots,l_1$ for an $A_4$--$A_3$ line and $x=g_2,\ldots,l_2$ for the
$A_3$--$A_3$ line); in each case the loop momentum is routed so that the
regulated line carries momentum $q$.
\end{quote}

\begin{quote}

the multiplicities follow the same counting as for $\lambda_{3A}$, with one
refinement for the gluon triangles. for the ghost and quark boxes
$8=4\times 2$ (4 positions of the insertion $\times$ 2 loop orientations),
